YouTube Video Summariser

Paste a video's transcript, or upload a downloaded transcript file, and get a summary of the video's key points.

Getting a transcript: on the video's YouTube page, click the "···" button below the video (or expand the description), choose Show transcript, then select all and copy the text in. No account or download needed.
